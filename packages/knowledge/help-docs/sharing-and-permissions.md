# Sharing folders and permissions

Each folder has an **owner**, and the owner can share it with other people.

| Role   | View records | Edit records | Approve bulk updates | Share folder |
|--------|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Owner  | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Editor | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Viewer | Yes | No  | No  | No |

## Share a folder
1. Open the folder and click **Share**.
2. Enter the person's email address and pick **Editor** or **Viewer**.
3. Click **Invite**.

The assistant always acts as you. It can only see and change folders you have access to, with your role.
