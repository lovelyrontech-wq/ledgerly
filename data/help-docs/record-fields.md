# Record fields

Every record has these fields:
- **Name**: invoice number or product name.
- **Value**: the amount or price, in the record's currency.
- **Currency**: for example USD or EUR. Bulk updates never convert currencies.
- **Status**: for invoices `draft`, `sent`, `paid`, or `overdue`; for products `active` or `archived`.

Invoices also have **customer**, **issued date**, and **due date**. Products have **SKU**, **stock**, and **category**.

## Edit one record
Click the record in the folder, change the field, and click **Save**.
