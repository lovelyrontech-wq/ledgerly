# Undo a bulk update and view history

## Undo
Every approved bulk update is saved as a **batch**. To reverse it:
- Ask the assistant: "undo the last change", or
- Open **Folder settings → History**, find the batch, and click **Undo**.

Undo puts back the values each record had before the batch. If a record was edited again after the batch, undo skips that record and tells you, so newer edits are not lost.

## History and audit log
**Folder settings → History** lists every preview, approval, and undo: who did it, when, and the before and after value of each record. The history cannot be edited or deleted.
